## Purpose

This directory documents Klugminds legal framework — contracts, compliance obligations, intellectual property, and regulatory requirements. Legal documentation protects the company and ensures public-facing content (including the company website) meets legal standards.

## Legal Domains

| Domain     | What this directory covers                               |

| Privacy    | Privacy policy, data processing, consent management      |
| Compliance | GDPR, CCPA, and industry-specific regulations            |
| Terms      | Terms of service, acceptable use, SLA definitions        |

## Website Legal Requirements

The company website must include:

- **Privacy policy** — how visitor data is collected and used
- **Terms of service** — conditions for using the site and services
- **Cookie policy** — if tracking cookies are used
- **Copyright notice** — consistent with [LICENSE](../../LICENSE) (MIT for code; separate notice for content)

Legal review should precede publication of any page making product claims or collecting user data.
